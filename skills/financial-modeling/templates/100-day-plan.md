# 100-Day Plan Framework (Post-LBO)

> LBO success is 90% determined by the first 100 days of execution.

## Day 1-30: Stabilization

**Goal**: Maintain business continuity, prevent talent loss and customer attrition.

| # | Activity | Owner | Deadline | Output | Financial Impact |
|---|---------|-------|---------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Board first meeting | CEO + Sponsor | Day 5 | Board charter, annual targets, governance | Governance framework |
| 2 | Key management retention agreements | HR + Sponsor | Day 10 | 3-5yr retention, equity acceleration | Retention risk ↓ |
| 3 | Customer communication plan | Sales VP + CEO | Day 7 | Visit scripts, renewal confirmations | Revenue protection |
| 4 | Supplier & creditor communication | CFO | Day 10 | Payment confirmation, no-change statement | Operational continuity |
| 5 | IT systems & data access audit | CTO + Sponsor | Day 3 | System audit, data backup, access list | Information access |
| 6 | Bank/creditor notification | CFO + Legal | Day 1 | Financing step certificates, covenant baseline | Lender trust |
| 7 | Internal communications | HR + Comms | Day 2 | All-hands, FAQ doc, hotline | Employee retention |
| 8 | Pre-funding steps | CFO + Finance | Day 20 | Payroll & OpEx pre-payment arrangements | Liquidity |

**Day 30 Milestones**:
- [ ] Key management (3-5 people) signed retention agreements, no unexpected departures
- [ ] Customer renewal rate >= 95%
- [ ] No supplier payment delays
- [ ] Bank covenant baseline confirmed
- [ ] Employee turnover < 2%

## Day 31-60: Diagnosis

**Goal**: Rapidly identify EBITDA improvement opportunities and build Year 1 plan foundation.

| Workstream | Method | Output | Est. Impact |
|-----------|--------|--------|-------------|
| A. EBITDA improvement scan | P&L analysis, industry benchmarking | Prioritized opportunity list | +3-5% margin |
| B. Cost structure optimization | SG&A waterfall, spend categorization | Cuttable costs list | -$1-2M SG&A/yr |
| C. Pricing power assessment | Product-level margins, competitive benchmarking | ASP increase targets | +$0.5-1.5M rev/yr |
| D. Capital efficiency review | CapEx history, maintenance vs growth split | Optimized CapEx plan | Release $2-3M cash/yr |
| E. Working capital optimization | DSO/DIO/DPO analysis, inventory aging | NWC release plan | One-time $3-8M cash |
| F. Revenue funnel analysis | Pipeline review, win rates, deal sizes | Growth opportunity map | +$1-3M rev/yr |

**Day 60 Milestones**:
- [ ] EBITDA improvement list finalized, total potential $4-8M
- [ ] Cost reduction initiated: hiring freeze, procurement negotiations started
- [ ] Pricing assessment complete with qualified ASP targets
- [ ] Working capital release plan confirmed, $3-5M expected by Day 100
- [ ] Year 1 financial target draft complete

## Day 61-100: Launch

**Goal**: Launch specific initiatives, establish 18-month milestone tracking.

### Initiative Tracker Template

```
Initiative: [Name] (Target: [financial impact])
├── Day __: [First action / milestone]
├── Day __: [Second action / milestone]
├── Day __: [Q1 run-rate achieved]
└── 18-month target: [Cumulative impact]
```

### Year 1 EBITDA Bridge

```
Entry EBITDA (LTM)                    $___M
Less: Execution Discount (-__%)       ($___M)
Base EBITDA (Year 1)                  $___M
+ Value Creation Initiatives          $___M
= Year 1 Projected EBITDA            $___M
```

**Day 100 Milestones**:
- [ ] All priority initiatives launched with named owners
- [ ] 18-month milestone tracker operational
- [ ] Year 1 budget finalized and board-approved
- [ ] Working capital cash released per plan
- [ ] Quarterly board reporting cadence established
