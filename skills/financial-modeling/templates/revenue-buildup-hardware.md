## The 30% Rule
Any single line > 30% of its parent product line MUST be split further with independent drivers.

## Validation
- [ ] Each hardware line has Geography + Channel identified
- [ ] No single geo/channel > 30% without sub-division
- [ ] All row sums = report-level revenue
- [ ] CAGR from line-level assumptions, not aggregate
