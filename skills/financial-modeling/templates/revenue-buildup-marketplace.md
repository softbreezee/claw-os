# Revenue Build-up Template: Marketplace / Platform

## Revenue Architecture (Customer-Dimension)
```
Revenue = Σ(by Supplier/Platform) [
  Beginning Accounts × (1 + New Account Growth %)
  × ARPU × (1 + ARPU Growth %)
]
```

Use customer-dimension when: company is reseller/distributor/agent AND top supplier > 30% of revenue.
